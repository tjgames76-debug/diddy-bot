Diddy Bot - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Diddy Bot handles information when you use the bot on Discord.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect as little information as possible.

2. Information We Collect

Diddy Bot does not intentionally collect or save personal information in a database.

The bot processes message content to detect the word "diddy" and determine when to send its picture response.

3. Information We Store

Diddy Bot does not maintain a database or intentionally save:

  • User messages
  • User IDs
  • Server IDs
  • Personal information
  • Message history

4. How Information Is Used

Message content is processed only to detect the trigger word and send the appropriate picture response.

We do not use message content for advertising or user profiling.

5. Third-Party Services

Diddy Bot operates through Discord. Discord may independently process information according to its own privacy practices.

Discord Privacy Policy: https://discord.com/privacy

The hosting provider used to run Diddy Bot may also process technical information or server logs.

6. Data Retention

Diddy Bot does not intentionally retain user messages or personal information in a database.

Any technical logs generated by Discord or the hosting provider are subject to their respective policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Diddy Bot does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the bot's functionality or data practices change.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the bot developer through Discord.
